LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2025,
by and between:

Landlord: Greenfield Property Management LLC
          200 Oak Avenue, Suite 10, Seattle, WA 98101

Tenant:   Christopher and Amanda Reeves
          (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant")

Property: 45 Birchwood Court, Apartment 3B, Seattle, WA 98105

1. TERM
   The lease term shall commence on March 1, 2025 and end on February 28, 2026
   (12 months), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2. RENT
   Monthly rent is $1,850.00, due on the 1st of each month.
   A late fee of $75.00 applies if rent is received after the 5th.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
   Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,700.00 (two months' rent) prior to
   move-in. The deposit will be held in a separate escrow account.

4. UTILITIES
   Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, and internet services.
   Water and trash collection are included in the rent.

5. MAINTENANCE
   Tenant agrees to maintain the property in good condition and promptly report
   any damage or required repairs to the Landlord.

6. PETS
   No pets are permitted without prior written consent from the Landlord.

7. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice.
